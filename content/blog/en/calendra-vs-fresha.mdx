Fresha is one of the largest salon booking platforms in the world. Its website lists more than 130,000 partner businesses in over 120 countries. Slovenian salons and studios therefore often compare it with local software.

## Prices and commissions

Fresha has two plans: Independent for one person at $19.95 a month and Team at $14.95 per bookable team member. Prices are in dollars and exclude local taxes; we did not find euro prices on the site, so check them when you sign up.

The main difference is the marketplace. When a new client finds you through Fresha's marketplace, Fresha charges a one-time 20% commission, at least $6. On a €50 service that is €10. Returning clients and bookings through your own link, Facebook or Instagram carry no commission. Online payments cost 2.79% + $0.20 per transaction.

Calendra charges no booking commissions. You pay for the plan, extra users (€5.90 a month), SMS as used (€0.05) and, if needed, the fiscal cash register (€9.90 a month). All prices exclude VAT and are on the [pricing page](/en/pricing).

## Slovenian language and fiscal cash register

A Slovenian business that accepts cash or cards must fiscally verify its invoices. Fresha's pages do not mention a Slovenian fiscal cash register or Slovenian language support, so you would likely need separate software for invoices.

Calendra issues an invoice directly from the appointment and fiscally verifies it with FURS when required. The app works in Slovenian and English. When fiscal verification is mandatory is explained in [fiscal verification of invoices](/en/blog/fiscal-verification-of-invoices-slovenia).

## Group classes and waiting lists

Fresha's group bookings let several clients, such as friends or family, book together and pay in one checkout. We did not find class capacity or class timetables on its pages.

Fresha's waiting list is well designed: when a slot opens, it is offered automatically to waiting clients. You choose whether the first in line, the highest-value client or the first to confirm gets it.

Calendra is also built for studios and classes. You set capacity, track registrations and attendance, and link memberships and class passes. When a class is full, extra clients join the waiting list and move into a place when one becomes available. More on [group bookings](/en/group-bookings).

## Online consultations

For psychologists, consultants and coaches, the client should receive the video-call link automatically. When an online appointment is confirmed, Calendra creates a Zoom link and includes it in the confirmation and reminders, and it also supports Google Meet. Both require a connected account and a suitable plan. Fresha lists Google, Facebook, Instagram and payment providers among its integrations, but not video consultations. More on the [Zoom integration](/en/zoom-integration) page.

## When to choose Fresha

- You run a salon in a city with many international visitors and want new clients from a global marketplace.
- You need Fresha's POS terminal for in-person payments and product sales.
- You do not need a Slovenian interface and issue invoices in another program.

## When to choose Calendra

- You need fiscally verified invoices in the same program as your appointments.
- You run group classes with capacity, memberships and a waiting list.
- You hold online sessions over Zoom or Google Meet.
- You do not want to pay commission on new clients and prefer prices in euros.

## How to decide

The most reliable test is to try both on one week of your own appointments. Fresha offers a 7-day trial and Calendra a 14-day trial with no credit card.
