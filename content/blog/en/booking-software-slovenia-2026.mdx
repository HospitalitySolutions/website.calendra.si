Every year there are more booking programs on the Slovenian market, and their prices are harder to compare. One quotes VAT-inclusive prices, another excludes VAT, a third uses dollars and a fourth charges for SMS separately. This comparison puts eight programs side by side.

The comparison was written by the Calendra team, so Calendra is one of the options in the table. Information about other vendors comes only from their official websites and was checked on 6 November 2026. Anything a vendor does not state is marked "not stated", not "no". Prices change, so check the vendor's current price list before you decide.

## How to compare prices

The published plan price is only the start of the calculation. Watch four things.

- **VAT.** Naroči se! quotes prices including VAT; Calendra and Lime Booking exclude VAT (22%). SpotDrop and Terminček do not say. €9.99 including VAT is about €8.19 excluding VAT.
- **Users.** Some plans include one employee, others unlimited staff. An extra user costs €5.90 a month at Calendra, €9.90 at Lime Booking and €4.90 at SpotDrop.
- **SMS.** Most vendors charge SMS per message or in bundles: €0.05 at Calendra, €0.06 at SpotDrop and Lime Booking. At 200 reminders a month that is €10 to €12 extra.
- **Currency and fees.** Micali, Zenamu and Fresha quote prices in dollars. Fresha also charges a commission on new clients from its marketplace, and Zenamu charges a card fee on its free plan.

A detailed monthly cost calculation is in [how much booking software costs](/en/blog/how-much-does-booking-software-cost).

## The programs one by one

### Calendra

Calendra is Slovenian software for service businesses that covers invoices, a fiscal cash register, group classes and online consultations alongside booking. Plans cost €15.90, €26.90 and €39.90 a month excluding VAT. Group bookings are available from the Professional plan; the waiting list and multiple locations are in Premium. Zoom and Google Meet require a connected account and a suitable plan. Current prices are on the [pricing page](/en/pricing).

### Naroči se!

Naroči se! has the longest free trial, 30 days, and plans from €9.99 including VAT for one location and one employee. It supports group appointments with a participant limit, a waiting list, invoices sent by email and online payments through PayPal, Stripe, Mollie and Square, depending on the plan. SMS is not included in the basic plans; plans with SMS start at €34.39 a month.

### Lime Booking

Lime Booking describes itself as a Slovenian app and has a built-in fiscal cash register that verifies invoices with FURS. Plans cost €14.90 to €69.90 a month excluding VAT. Group appointments are in the Napredni plan and the waiting list in Growth. It can send notifications by SMS, email, WhatsApp and Viber.

### SpotDrop

SpotDrop is the cheapest paid option here, with a Basic plan at €6.99 a month for one employee. When a slot frees up, a client on the waiting list automatically receives an offer by email. Group appointments, invoicing and a fiscal cash register are not described on its page.

### Terminček and NeoBiz

Terminček costs €9.90 a month with unlimited staff; SMS is pay-as-you-go. Invoices and the fiscal cash register come from NeoBiz, a separate program run by a different company (its Blagajna plan is €15 a month excluding VAT), which recommends Terminček for booking.

### Micali

Micali offers a free plan with online booking and email reminders, and SMS plus extra features for $10 a month. It supports appointments with capacity and appointment types for online consultations. Its Slovenian page does not describe invoicing or a fiscal cash register.

### Zenamu

Zenamu is built for studios and instructors: classes with capacity, memberships, class passes and a client app. The free plan includes one instructor account and up to 30 orders a month; paid plans start at $29 a month. The website is in English and Slovenian is not listed.

### Fresha

Fresha is an international salon platform with a marketplace where new clients can find you. For those clients it charges a one-time 20% commission, at least $6. It has an automatic waiting list and group bookings, but does not list Slovenian language support or a fiscal cash register. A detailed comparison is in [Calendra vs Fresha](/en/blog/calendra-vs-fresha).

Other vendors exist, such as MyPlanly, which we could not check because its website does not allow automated reading.

## What to choose for your business

**A salon with one or two employees.** If you only need online booking and reminders, SpotDrop, Terminček and Naroči se! are the cheapest start. Check what SMS will cost at your number of appointments.

**A salon that issues invoices.** If clients pay by card or cash, you need fiscal verification. Calendra and Lime Booking include it in the same program; with Terminček you get it through NeoBiz. More in [fiscal verification of invoices](/en/blog/fiscal-verification-of-invoices-slovenia).

**A yoga, Pilates or group-class studio.** You need capacity, a waiting list and class passes. Calendra, Lime Booking, Naroči se! and Zenamu offer these. For Calendra, see [group bookings](/en/group-bookings).

**Consultants, psychologists and coaches with online sessions.** An automatic video-call link matters. Calendra connects with Zoom and Google Meet; we did not find this on other Slovenian vendors' pages. More on the [Zoom integration](/en/zoom-integration) page.

## What to check before you decide

1. Whether the price includes or excludes VAT.
2. How many users, locations and SMS messages the plan includes.
3. Whether the program issues fiscally verified invoices, if you need them.
4. Whether you can export client data if you ever switch.
5. Whether support is available in Slovenian.
