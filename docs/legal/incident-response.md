# Personal-data incident response — operational procedure

Version 0.4 / 23 October 2026. Owner: Calendra operator, David Mirc. Published reporting channel: info@calendra.si. A substitute responder and out-of-band contact still need assignment; this document is not evidence of staffed 24/7 coverage. Statutory breach obligations are not paused by the 08:00–20:00 support window.

1. **Detect and record.** Start a restricted incident record. Capture detection time, awareness time, source, impacted services, tenant scope and known facts. Preserve relevant logs lawfully. Do not post client records, credentials or incident particulars in this PUBLIC repository.
2. **Contain safely.** Stop unauthorised access, revoke compromised credentials/sessions, isolate the affected route or resource and preserve evidence. Do not erase evidence or restore backups blindly. Record changes and decision owners.
3. **Assess.** Establish confidentiality, integrity and availability impact, affected individuals/records, likely consequences, sensitive information/minors and containment effectiveness. Distinguish suspicion from a reasonable degree of certainty that a personal-data breach occurred. Record reasons; do not wait for every detail before reaching awareness or notifying.
4. **Processor notification.** Notify each affected tenant/controller without undue delay after awareness. Initial notice may be incomplete. Give facts known, unknowns and next update mechanism. Do not wait for a completed investigation or treat 72 hours as a processor grace period.
5. **Controller duties.** For Calendra's own controller processing, assess regulator notice without undue delay and where feasible within 72 hours unless risk is unlikely. Explain delay if any. Assess communication to individuals without undue delay where high risk is likely, subject to statutory exceptions. Assist tenant decisions without taking over their role by default.
6. **Recover and validate.** Restore only from appropriate sources, reapply valid deletion instructions, verify permissions and test containment. Keep tenant updates factual; do not claim full containment without evidence.
7. **Close and improve.** Record facts, effects, risk evaluation, notification decision including any non-notification, remediation and lessons. Assign follow-up actions and retention/access rules. Keep evidence outside public source control.

## Tenant initial notice template

Subject: Personal-data breach notification — [incident reference]

Awareness date/time and zone: [value]
Affected tenant/service: [value]
Nature of breach and current known scope: [facts; distinguish unknowns]
Data/individual categories and approximate numbers: [known or investigating]
Likely consequences: [assessment]
Measures taken/proposed and recommended tenant action: [facts]
Further information contact: info@calendra.si / [verified alternative]
Next update: [agreed mechanism; no invented resolution promise]

Do not delay the initial message to fill every field. Deliver securely to the authorised tenant contact and log dispatch. Share only the tenant's own relevant facts.

## Individual notice template, where required

Explain plainly what happened, which of the person's data may be affected, likely consequences, actions taken, practical protection steps and a contact. Avoid speculation, minimisation of risk or claims of harmlessness without evidence. Coordinate with the relevant controller and authority while respecting notification urgency.
